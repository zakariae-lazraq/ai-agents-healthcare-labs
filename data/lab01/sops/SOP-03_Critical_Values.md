# SOP-03 — Critical value notification

*Northbridge Clinical & Research Laboratory — FICTIONAL document written for training. Not for clinical use.*

*Version 5.1 · Effective 2026-02-01 · Owner: Clinical Biochemistry · Approved: Laboratory Director*

## 1. Purpose

This procedure defines the critical values that require immediate notification, who is notified, within which time limit, and how the notification is documented.

## 2. Critical value list

Potassium below 2.8 or above 6.2 mmol/L. Sodium below 120 or above 160 mmol/L. Glucose below 2.5 or above 25.0 mmol/L. Total calcium below 1.6 or above 3.5 mmol/L. Haemoglobin below 60 g/L. Platelets below 20 × 10⁹/L. Only the analytes on this list are critical values.

## 3. Who to call and time limits

Inpatients: the responsible clinician or the nurse in charge of the ward, by telephone, within 30 minutes of result validation. Outpatients: the on-call GP service within 60 minutes. If nobody can be reached within these time limits, escalate to the laboratory medical director.

## 4. Read-back and documentation

The person receiving the call reads the result back. The notification is documented in the laboratory information system: name and role of the person notified, date and time, and confirmation of read-back.
