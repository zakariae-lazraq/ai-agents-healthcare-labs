# SOP-01 — 25-hydroxyvitamin D (D2 + D3) by LC-MS/MS

*Northbridge Clinical & Research Laboratory — FICTIONAL document written for training. Not for clinical use.*

*Version 4.2 · Effective 2026-03-01 · Owner: Mass Spectrometry Section · Approved: Laboratory Director*

## 1. Purpose and scope

This procedure describes the quantification of 25-hydroxyvitamin D2 and 25-hydroxyvitamin D3 in human serum by liquid chromatography–tandem mass spectrometry (LC-MS/MS). It applies to all routine and research samples analysed on the Northbridge LC-MS/MS platform.

## 2. Specimen

Serum collected in a serum separator tube (SST). Minimum sample volume: 0.5 mL of serum. Acceptance, rejection and stability criteria are defined in SOP-02 (Specimen acceptance and rejection).

## 3. Calibration

Six calibrators covering 5 to 150 ng/mL are analysed at the start of every batch. The calibration curve is linear with 1/x weighting. Acceptance: coefficient of determination r² ≥ 0.995, and the back-calculated concentration of each calibrator within ±15 % of its nominal value (±20 % for the lowest calibrator). A batch whose calibration fails these criteria must not be reported.

## 4. Internal standard

The internal standard is deuterated 25-hydroxyvitamin D3 (d6). The internal standard peak area of every sample must lie between 50 % and 150 % of the mean internal standard area of the calibrators and quality controls of the same batch. A sample outside this window is re-injected; if it fails again, it is re-extracted.

## 5. Ion ratio

For each analyte, the ratio of qualifier to quantifier transition must lie within ±20 % of the mean ion ratio of the calibrators of the same batch. A sample outside this tolerance is put on hold and investigated for interference; it is not reported until the investigation is closed.

## 6. Quality control

Three quality control levels (low 15 ng/mL, mid 50 ng/mL, high 120 ng/mL) are analysed in duplicate in every batch. Westgard rules are applied to the QC results: 1-3s, 2-2s, R-4s, 4-1s and 10-x are rejection rules; 1-2s is a warning rule. The 2-2s rule rejects the run when two consecutive QC results exceed 2 SD on the same side of the mean. Any rejection rule violation stops reporting and triggers a deviation report.

## 7. Reporting

The result is reported as total 25-hydroxyvitamin D (D2 + D3) in ng/mL. The analytical measuring range is 5 to 150 ng/mL. Results below 5 ng/mL are reported as "< 5 ng/mL". Samples above 150 ng/mL are diluted 1:2 with vitamin D–stripped serum and re-analysed. The laboratory does not provide treatment or dosing advice; clinical questions are referred to the requesting clinician.

## 8. Records

Batch records, chromatograms and QC data are retained for 2 years.
